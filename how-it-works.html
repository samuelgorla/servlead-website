An AI build

03 / When it's a build

Agentic AI at the top. Every piece real and running.

When the fix is a build, this is the range — newest and most capable at the top. Every one is a live n8n workflow, not a slide. I build for efficiency and price: the least complex thing that solves the leak, so you get the result without paying for machinery you don't need.

● The top of the line — agentic AI

A fleet that runs itself.

Not a single automation waiting to be told what to do — several agents that hand work to each other. One finds the task, another does it, another files the result. You approve only what matters.

This isn't theory. I run a chief-of-staff agent in my own business: after I visit a company, I tell it who I saw, and it writes the CRM and flags that company so I don't double back — work I used to do at night. When a vendor sent a gift card to the wrong inbox, the same agent found the order, filed the resend, and tracked it to resolution. I approved the one step that mattered and skipped the chat queue.

The question was never "should I use agents." It's "which of my repeating tasks is worth handing off first." That's the smaller question — and it's the one that pays.
