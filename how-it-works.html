02 / Inside the fix

Four kinds of answer. Not AI for the sake of it.

The plan's honest output is whatever solves the leak. Four kinds of answer show up. Most often it's the first — but only when it's genuinely the right one.

● Most of what I do

An AI build

Something built to do the repetitive work on its own. Where the every-week manual tasks go away — and where agentic AI comes in, below.

Sometimes cheaper

A tool

Sometimes the fix is software that already exists — occasionally one you're already paying for and not using. When that's the answer, I say so, and it costs you nothing more.

Sometimes a person

A new hire

Sometimes the work is real and needs a person — just not you, or not the one doing it now. The plan says so plainly.

Sometimes free

A process fix

Sometimes nothing needs buying or building. The steps just need reordering so the work stops falling on the floor.

03 / When it's a build

Two flagships, five building blocks. All of it running.

When the fix is a build, this is the range, most capable at the top. Every piece is running today, not a slide. The build is always the least complex thing that solves the leak, so you get the result without paying for machinery you don't need.

● Flagship · agentic AI

A chief of staff that runs on a clock.

Not an automation waiting to be told what to do. It's an agent that knows the business, works inside the tools you already use, and handles the jobs that repeat on a schedule. It drafts. You approve.

This isn't theory. I run one in my own business. After I visit a company, I tell it who I saw, and it writes the CRM and flags that company so I don't double back. That's work I used to do at night. When a vendor sent a gift card to the wrong inbox, the same agent found the order, filed the resend, and tracked it to resolution. I approved the one step that mattered and skipped the chat queue.

The question was never "should I use agents." It's "which of my repeating tasks is worth handing off first." That's the smaller question, and it's the one that pays.
