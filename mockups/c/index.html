What happens on the free call?

You pick one or two workflows that eat your week. You walk me through how they run today, I ask plain questions, and I show you what I see and the direction I'd take. It's 30 minutes, free, by phone or video, or in person in the Lehigh Valley.

Do I need the audit before a rebuild?

No. The call leads to one of three equal paths: a Workflow Rebuild, the AI & Efficiency Audit, or a straight answer. They aren't steps in a sequence. I'll recommend the one that fits.

What does it cost?

The free call costs nothing. A Workflow Rebuild is a fixed price, quoted after the call. The AI & Efficiency Audit is $1,000: $500 to book, $500 when the plan lands. If I don't show you at least $10,000 a year leaking out of your business, audit is free.

Do you work outside the Lehigh Valley?

Yes. I'm based in the Lehigh Valley and work with owners here and beyond. Phone and video work anywhere.

What about client or patient data?

I only handle sensitive data with the right safeguards in place, and I'll tell you up front if your situation needs more than I can responsibly provide.

What if it's not a fit?

You get a straight answer, even if that answer isn't me. If a tool you already pay for handles it, or I'm not the right person for it, I'll tell you and point you to who is.
