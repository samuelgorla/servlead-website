AI & Efficiency Audit

AIG Safety

AIG Safety (Access Innovations Global) makes safety products and sells them through a dealer network of about 50 dealers. A lean team of four runs the business. This is what the audit found.

  • Orders were entered by hand, and shipping documents were typed in again, because QuickBooks didn't connect to the shipping carriers. About 6 hours a week.
  • Dealers send purchase orders in their own formats and their own part numbers. Those can be mapped to the manufacturer's part numbers automatically. Designed, not built.
  • Collections follow-up and inventory visibility were weak spots.
