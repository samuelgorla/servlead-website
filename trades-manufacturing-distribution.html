03 / Distributors

Orders still get keyed in by hand.

Wholesale and supply houses.

Dealer paperwork gets keyed in by hand too. Unpaid invoices get chased by hand. There's no clear view of which dealers are active.

From a real AI & Efficiency Audit

AIG Safety

AIG Safety (Access Innovations Global) makes safety products and sells them through a dealer network of about 50 dealers. A lean team runs it.

  • Orders were entered by hand, and shipping documents were re-keyed, because QuickBooks didn't connect to the carriers. About 6 hours a week.
  • Dealers send purchase orders in their own formats and their own part numbers. A mapping to the manufacturer's part numbers was designed, not built.
  • Collections follow-up and inventory visibility were weak spots.
