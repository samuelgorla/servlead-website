    The slips stand in for the people on the slider. They are not a headcount of a real company. Slide past 250, or type a larger team.

    hours × people × cost × weeks = estimated yearly leak

    Example only, not a benchmark. 3 hours a week × 25 people × $46.89/hr × 48 weeks = $168,804 a year.

    1. In private industry an employee hour costs $46.89 in total compensation, and it rises with employer size: $37.64 at 1–49 workers, $47.96 at 100–499, $67.68 at 500+. Source: BLS ECEC June 2026, https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ecec.t06.htm
    2. Benefits are about 30% of what an hour really costs. Source: BLS ECEC, https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ecec.nr0.htm
    3. Knowledge workers were estimated to spend about a fifth of their week searching for and gathering information. Source: McKinsey Global Institute, cited in 'The economic potential of generative AI' (2023), https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/mckinsey-digital/our-insights/the-economic-potential-of-generative-ai-the-next-productivity-frontier

    One working day, from the phone already on to the hour the inbox should have closed. The calculator is an on-screen graphic. The citations run as end notes under that graphic. Proof is a contact sheet, not a card grid.

    Palette note: the field is #0C121B, a darker exposure of navy #14202E, so the day can sit in a theater. It is the same navy with the lights down, not a new brand color. Gold is the practical light. Rust is only the booking button. Fonts stay Newsreader, Archivo, and Spline Sans Mono, set large. The live logo check is green, so this direction uses a gold tick. Client logos are unchanged.

    Motion: scraps drift until you tap them, then they settle. A timecode follows the chapter. The head’s contour draws once. Reduced motion holds every scrap still and the contour already drawn.
