Three directions for the homepage.

Servlead Solutions. Sam Gorla, Lehigh Valley. Owner-run companies from about $2M up to $20M–$50M. The business still runs through the owner’s head. These pages are previews, not a build. The live homepage is untouched.

Each direction has the same words for the scale of a leak, the same formula, and the same three cited facts. Only the layout changes. Proof uses the four permitted clients. Audit figures stay separate and are not added together.

The live mark’s check is green. These directions redraw that tick in gold so the pages we design introduce no green. Client logos stay as given, including the green in the S.M. Klase mark. Fonts stay Newsreader, Archivo, and Spline Sans Mono. Direction C darkens navy for the theater and says so on the page. Rust appears only as the single booking button inside each direction.

Direction A

Field Notes

The homepage is a bound notebook. The problem is an entry, the leaks are lines you mark, and the scale math is a sum on the same page. A busy owner can read one spread. The cited facts sit with the sum.

Navy ink on warm paper. Gold rules. Interactive: lane tabs, struck leaks, a people slider that repeats the mark, and the formula.

Direction B

Sheet A1

The business is a plan, and every wing still passes through the owner, from about $2M up to $20M–$50M. You light the leaks on the drawing. The quantity takeoff runs the same formula.

Navy field, slate grid, gold for anything lit. Interactive: rooms that light, occupant marks, and the takeoff.

Direction C

The Day

One working day, from the phone already on to the hour the inbox should have closed. The calculator is an on-screen graphic. Proof is a contact sheet. The citations sit under the graphic.

A darker exposure of the same navy, stated on the page. Interactive: scraps you tap, a timecode, and the people slider.
