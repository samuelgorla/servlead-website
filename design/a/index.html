    The same leak gets heavier as the company gets larger. The next page of the notebook is how it adds up, from numbers you enter.

    Most leaks start small: a missed call, a slow quote, a handoff that slips through the cracks. As your business grows, those same leaks repeat across more people, more locations and more calls, quotes and appointments, and each hour tends to cost more at larger employers too. I won't guess a dollar figure for your business, but I'll show you how to measure yours with your own numbers.

    See how one leak adds up

    The marks follow the people on the slider. They are not a headcount of a real company.

    Each mark is one person on the slider.

    hours × people × cost × weeks = estimated yearly leak

    Example only, not a benchmark. 3 hours a week × 25 people × $46.89/hr × 48 weeks = $168,804 a year.

    1. In private industry an employee hour costs $46.89 in total compensation, and it rises with employer size: $37.64 at 1–49 workers, $47.96 at 100–499, $67.68 at 500+. Source: BLS ECEC June 2026, https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ecec.t06.htm
    2. Benefits are about 30% of what an hour really costs. Source: BLS ECEC, https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ecec.nr0.htm
    3. Knowledge workers were estimated to spend about a fifth of their week searching for and gathering information. Source: McKinsey Global Institute, cited in 'The economic potential of generative AI' (2023), https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/mckinsey-digital/our-insights/the-economic-potential-of-generative-ai-the-next-productivity-frontier

    The homepage is a bound notebook. The problem is an entry, the leaks are lines you mark, and the scale math is a sum on the same page. A busy owner can read one spread. The cited facts sit in the margin of the sum.

    Palette: navy ink on warm paper. Gold is a rule and a mark, not body text. Rust is only the booking button. The live mark’s check is green, so this direction uses a wordmark and a gold tick. Client logos stay as they are, including the green in the S.M. Klase mark. Fonts unchanged: Newsreader, Archivo, Spline Sans Mono.

    Motion: the notebook lines draw in. A marked leak takes a gold tick on a warm wash. The people slider repeats the mark, and above 36 people each mark stands for 10. Reduced motion keeps every line already drawn.
