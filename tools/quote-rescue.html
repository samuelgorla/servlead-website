Questions people ask

What does this write?

One short follow-up from a quote, proposal, or engagement letter that went quiet. It checks in, and it says you will close the quote out unless you hear back. You copy it and send it yourself.

Will it invent a price, a date, or a discount?

It is instructed not to add a price, a date, or a discount, and the page checks the draft for those before showing it. It can still miss something. Read the note before you send it. If a name or a detail is missing, it leaves a [bracket] for you to fill in.

Is my quote stored?

No. Your text is sent to an AI model to write the drafts and isn't stored by us. Remove names or prices first if you prefer. If you leave your email, that email, your first name, and your business type go to Sam. The quote does not.

Who sends the follow-up?

You do. This page never emails your customer.

How many can I run?

About five a day from one connection. It is a simple limit, not a precise one. If you hit it, the page will say so.

What if I want this on every quote?
