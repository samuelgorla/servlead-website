    Questions people ask

    How is the number calculated?

    Quotes per month, times 12, times the share that go quiet, times the close rate, times your average job value. Then I round down. The page shows each step with your own numbers so you can check it.

    Where does the 10% close rate come from?

    It's an assumption, not a statistic. I picked a low number on purpose so the result doesn't oversell. If you know how often a follow-up brings a job back in your business, type that in instead.

    Is this profit or revenue?

    Revenue. It's the sales value of the work, before your costs. Apply your own margin to see what it means for profit.

    What counts as a quote that went quiet?

    One where the customer didn't answer and nobody on your side followed up. If they said no, or you followed up and still heard nothing, leave it out.

    I send proposals or engagement letters, not quotes. Does this still work?

    Yes. The math is the same for a contractor's estimate, a distributor's quote, or a CPA's engagement letter. Use whatever you send and the average value of the work when it closes.

    Do you save my numbers?

    No. The math runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to me or stored.
