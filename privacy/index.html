Servlead Solutions LLC (“Servlead,” “I,” “me”) is a one-person consulting practice based in Orefield, Pennsylvania. This policy explains what information I collect, why, and what I do with it.

Who I am

Servlead Solutions LLC is owned and operated solely by Samuel Gorla. There is no staff, no account management team, and no third party who handles your information on my behalf beyond the service providers named below.

What I collect

When you book a call. Name, email address, phone number, company name, and anything you type into the booking form. Bookings run through Cal.com and land on my Microsoft 365 calendar.

When you fill out an intake form. Whatever you choose to share about your business — team size, tools you use, where your process breaks down. Forms are hosted on Tally.

When you pay. Payments are processed by Stripe. I never see or store your full card number or bank account details. I see your name, email, the amount, and whether it cleared.

When you email or call me. The content of what you send, held in Microsoft 365.

When you visit this website. Aggregate traffic data through Cloudflare Web Analytics — page views, referring site, country, browser, device type. This tool does not use cookies and does not fingerprint or track you across other websites. I cannot identify you from it.

If I contact you first. Business contact information — name, job title, company, work email, business phone — sourced from public listings and business data providers. This is standard business-to-business prospecting. If you'd rather not hear from me, say so once and I'll remove you.

Recordings

Pennsylvania requires the consent of everyone on a call before it can be recorded. I ask out loud, every time, before recording anything — in person, on video, or by phone. If you say no, I take notes instead and it changes nothing about the work.

Recordings and transcripts are used to write your report accurately. They're stored in my Plaud account and deleted on request.

What I do with it

  • Deliver the audit, the written plan, and any project work you engage me for
  • Send you the report and follow up about your engagement
  • Process payment
  • Occasionally reach out to businesses I think I can help

I do not sell your information. I do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. I do not name you publicly as a client — no case study, testimonial, logo, or social post — without your explicit written permission first.

Service providers

I use these companies to run the business, and your information passes through them. Each has its own privacy policy.

  • Cloudflare · hosting, analytics
  • Cal.com · booking
  • Microsoft 365 · email, calendar, video
  • Stripe · payments
  • Tally · forms
  • Notion · client notes, project records
  • Plaud · recording, transcription
  • Anthropic · AI-assisted drafting and analysis
  • BoldSign · e-signatures

On AI specifically: I use AI tools in my work, including to help analyze notes and draft documents. Business information you share may be processed by those tools. I do not use client information to train AI models, and the providers I use offer the same commitment for business accounts.

What I don't take

I do not accept, store, or process protected health information, consumer financial account data, or other regulated personal data. I don't currently hold the compliance infrastructure that kind of work requires, and I'd rather say that plainly than work around it. If your project would involve regulated data, tell me early and I'll be straight with you about whether I can take it on.

How long I keep things

Client project records and reports: seven years, for tax and professional reasons.

Recordings and transcripts: until the engagement closes, or sooner on request.

Prospect contact information: removed on request, and I keep a suppression record so I don't accidentally contact you again.

Website analytics: retained by Cloudflare on their schedule. It isn't tied to you personally.

Your choices

Ask me and I'll tell you what I have about you, correct anything wrong, delete it, or stop contacting you. No form to fill out — email or call.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state or national privacy law. I'll honor those requests regardless of whether the law technically applies to a business my size.

Children

This is a business-to-business service. It isn't directed at anyone under 18, and I don't knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes get communicated directly to active clients.
