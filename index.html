Governance & security

How I keep your data safe

  • Only the data the job needs. Individual logins with two-step sign-in. No shared passwords.
  • Patient records only with the right safeguards in place. No tax-return or legal-matter files in AI tools.
When something goes wrong, and what waits for a human
  • If something goes wrong, I pause it, tell you the same day, fix it, and write down what changed.
  • If you stop working with me, I remove my access, change the keys, and hand over everything.
  • Prices, discounts, scope changes and complaints go to you, or the person you name.
  • Anything that sends, posts, deletes, pays or buys waits for a yes until you sign off on letting routine items run.
  • If a fact is missing, the draft leaves a [bracket]. The AI doesn't guess. When something looks wrong, it stops and asks.
