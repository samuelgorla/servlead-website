● Named, not priced

A third of what I find gets no number at all.

Eight of the twenty-four leaks were named and left unpriced on purpose. One was a hundred thousand dollars in aged invoices — real, and still not a yearly loss, so it doesn't get counted like one. Another was a sub-roster one trade deep; the honest fix there is relationships, not software.

Forcing a figure onto those would have made the reports look better and been worth less. If I can't defend a number in front of your accountant, you get the leak without the number.
