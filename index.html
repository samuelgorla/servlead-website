Before you say yes

Three things owners ask me first.

Business

Does it pay?

  • $38K–$54K a year shown at Kahler Construction.
  • Five stars from all four Google reviews.
  • If I don't show you at least $10,000 a year leaking out of your business, audit is free.

Technical

If there's a build, how is it built?

  • Tools you already pay for first. New ones only when they earn it.
  • Runs in accounts you own, in your business's name.
  • Prices, totals and dates come from your records. AI only reads and drafts.
  • After handoff, your team runs it. You get a written page for what was built and a copy of it in your own files.

Governance & security

How I keep your data safe

  • Only the data the job needs. Individual logins with two-step sign-in. No shared passwords.
  • Patient records only with the right safeguards in place. No tax-return or legal-matter files in AI tools.
  • If something goes wrong, I pause it, tell you the same day, fix it, and write down what changed.
  • If you stop working with me, I remove my access, change the keys, and hand over everything.
What always waits for a human
  • Prices, discounts, scope changes and complaints go to you, or the person you name.
  • Anything that sends, posts, deletes, pays or buys waits for a yes until you sign off on letting routine items run.
  • If a fact is missing, the draft leaves a [bracket]. The AI doesn't guess.
  • When something looks wrong, it stops and asks. It doesn't send.
