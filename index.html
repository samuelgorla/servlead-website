The question behind each stage

The AI & Efficiency Audit follows the money through four stages of your business. Watch the 31-second version below, or read the question I ask at each stage.

Getting customers

Where does new work actually come from? What happens to an enquiry that arrives at seven on a Friday night? We time the pipeline end to end.

Serving customers

What does delivering the work look like hour by hour? Who's the only person who can do each step — and what happens the week they're out?

Getting paid

How long from finishing the work to money in the bank? How much is sitting unpaid right now, and who's actually chasing it?

Running the back office

What admin burns hours every week that shouldn't need a person at all — hiring, scheduling, re-keying, reporting, follow-up nobody owns?

A third of what I show you gets no number at all.
